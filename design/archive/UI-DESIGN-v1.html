Flat colors. Oversize editorial typography. Fine rules. Real product screens, cropped into purposeful layers. Technical details stay in small monospace labels; benefits stay in plain language.

Motion direction

  1. First entrance: restrained text and product-frame reveal.
  2. 5.4-second scan: sweep → detect → plot → score. Pause and replay controls.
  3. Detection steps: user-controlled screenshot transitions.
  4. Comparison filters: animate the selected group on the target.
  5. Respect reduced motion. Film opens only on request with native video controls.

Responsive direction

At 760px, collapse to one column, use 24px gutters and a compact menu. Keep actual UI details legible and touch targets at least 44px. The layout on this design board responds to window size.

Research

Primary design references: Hoyt, Mathews, Easton, Linear. Specific findings and implementation decisions are recorded in the companion Markdown brief.

Research decisions · 04 Nov 2026

  • Mathews: actual homepage visual inspection supported real product imagery and a concise statement rather than feature-card clutter.
  • Hoyt and Easton: practical tuning and component vocabulary supports end, groups, shot order, setup, and real equipment details.
  • Linear: actual homepage visual inspection supported restrained typography, fine separators and large product UI.
  • Claude: warm-white header and solid dark CTA were observed; incomplete body loading limits conclusions.

These references inform the design; they are not partners, endorsements, or a survey of all American archers.
